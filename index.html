👻 Haunted Sylhet - Ghost Map

Tap a red pin to read the ghost story. Made in Sylhet.

1. MC College - Old Building

Students say at night, footsteps are heard in empty classrooms. Lights turn on by themselves.

2. Shahjalal University - Hill Area

Locals say a shadow walks near the hills after midnight. Many students avoid this road alone.

3. Keane Bridge - Surma River

Fishermen say they hear crying near the bridge at 3 AM. Old people say don't stop on the bridge at night.

4. Osmani Medical - Old Ward

Nurses say a lady in white saree is seen in the corridor. She disappears when you go close.

5. Malnicherra Tea Estate

Workers say at night, someone calls their name from the tea garden, but no one is there.
